title: "Privacy policy: gws-readonly"
---

gws-readonly is a personal command-line application used only by its owner, Jonny Mearns (jonny.mearns@gmail.com).

It uses read-only access to the owner's own Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Contacts. It cannot send email or create, change or delete any data.

Data is processed only on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared or disclosed to anyone.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
